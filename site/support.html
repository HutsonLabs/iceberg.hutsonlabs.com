Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and — if you enable sync — in your personal iCloud account. Iceberg has no servers of its own and no account to sign up for. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I sync between my Mac, iPhone, and iPad?

Sign in to the same Apple ID on each device and make sure iCloud is enabled for Iceberg in system Settings. Sync happens automatically through CloudKit.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Your content is yours — Iceberg supports exporting your notes and journal, and your data is never locked behind a proprietary server.

Something looks wrong after an update.

Quit and relaunch the app first; most glitches clear immediately. If not, email us with the version number and a screenshot and we'll dig in.

How do I join the beta?

Iceberg betas ship through Apple's TestFlight. Email us and we'll send you an invite link.

How do agents / MCP connections work?

Iceberg can act as a Model Context Protocol server so AI assistants you configure can read and add to your knowledge graph, locally, with your permission. Email us for setup help.
