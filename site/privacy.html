Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how HutsonLabs ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Iceberg app for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS (the "App") and this website. The short version: Iceberg is local-first, and we do not collect your notes.

Information we do not collect

Iceberg does not require an account. We do not operate servers that receive, store, or process the content you create in the App. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your notes, journal entries, books, captures, attachments, or knowledge graph.
  • Your name, email address, or contact information (unless you email us — see below).
  • Advertising identifiers. Iceberg contains no ads and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Where your data lives

  • On your device. Everything you create in Iceberg is stored locally on your Mac, iPhone, or iPad.
  • In your iCloud. If you enable sync, your data is synced between your devices through your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. That data is governed by your Apple ID and Apple's privacy policy; we cannot access it.

Device permissions

Iceberg asks for certain permissions only to power features you invoke, and only with your consent through the standard system prompts:

  • Location — to tag notes with the place they were written and power the Places view. Location data is stored with your notes on your device / in your iCloud, never sent to us.
  • Microphone — to record voice memos you capture. Recordings stay with your data.
  • Photos / camera — to attach images you choose to your notes.

You can change or revoke any permission at any time in system Settings; the related feature simply stops working.

AI and agent features

Iceberg can expose your knowledge graph to AI agents and assistants you configure (for example, via the Model Context Protocol). These connections are off by default, run under your control, and share data only with the services you choose to connect. We are not a party to, and do not receive data from, those connections. Please review the privacy terms of any AI provider you connect.

Website

This website is a static site served by Cloudflare. We do not run analytics or set cookies. Cloudflare may log standard technical request data (such as IP addresses) to operate the service; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Support email

If you contact us at hutson@qflow.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to respond to you, and we do not add you to mailing lists or share it with anyone.

Children's privacy

Iceberg is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we collect no personal data through the App, no such data is retained by us.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your devices and in your iCloud account, you are in full control of it. Deleting content in the App, or deleting the App and its iCloud data (Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage), removes it entirely. We hold nothing to delete on our end.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Since we do not collect personal data through the App, these rights are effectively satisfied by the controls above. For anything relating to a support email you sent us, contact us and we will promptly honor your request.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy — for example, if a future feature introduces any data collection — we will update this page and the "last updated" date, and describe the change in the App's release notes before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hutson@qflow.com.
